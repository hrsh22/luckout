Most problems sort themselves out, or take one tap to fix. Find yours below.

## A code doesn't work

- **Check the country.** A card works only in its own country and currency, unless it says it works worldwide.
- **Check how to use it.** The card's page in the shop has the brand's **How to redeem** and **Terms and validity**.
- **Copy it exactly.** Use the **Copy** button next to each part of the code in [Purchases](/purchases), and include the PIN if there is one.

If it still doesn't work, Bitrefill can help with a bad code: it emailed the code and its receipt to your [Checkout Email](/docs/sign-in#your-email-for-cards), and can reach you there. Luckout sources its gift cards from Bitrefill, which isn't affiliated with Luckout.

## A purchase is taking long

| The purchase says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Getting your code** | Wait a minute or two: the page updates by itself. |
| **Taking longer than usual** | It's safe. If it isn't delivered within 24 hours of buying, you can [reclaim the full amount](/docs/refunds#reclaim). |
| **Reclaim it** | Tap it and approve: the full amount goes back to your balance. |
| **Getting it ready** | If you haven't been charged, you can tap **Cancel**. Otherwise it's delivered or refunded in full. |
| **Refund on its way** | Nothing to do: Luckout's wallet pays it into your balance as soon as it can. See [Part of a refund on its way](/docs/refunds#part-of-a-refund-on-its-way). |
| **Your card is delivered, but its Draw didn't run** | Tap **Run the Draw**. It's free. |

## A Top-Up hasn't landed

Open your [wallet page](/wallet) and find it under **Money on its way**. It says where it's at:

- **Still waiting for your transfer:** if you've sent it, paste your transaction's hash into the box under it.
- **Taking longer than usual:** it's safe. 1Click either delivers it or sends it back.
- **Sent back:** the page says where it went. If it's in your NEAR Intents account, send it to your wallet or move it into your balance. See [Top-Up refunds](/docs/refunds#top-up-refunds).

## You can't buy

The Buy button says what's missing: funds, a limit, or your email for cards. [Buy a card](/docs/buy#if-you-cant-buy-yet) lists each one. If the shop says purchases are paused, your balance is safe and withdrawals still work: try again in a few minutes.

## The bot doesn't answer

- **It says it's offline:** its enclave check isn't passing right now, so it stays quiet rather than send unverified replies. You can still buy in the [shop](/shop).
- **It says that's a lot of messages:** it takes up to 10 a minute. Wait a minute.
- **It says to link your account first:** see [Link Telegram](/docs/telegram#link-telegram).
