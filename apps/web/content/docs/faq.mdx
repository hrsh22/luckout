## Cards

<Q q="Do I always get the card?">
  Yes. The Draw only decides how much cashback you get. If a card can't be delivered, the whole amount goes back to your balance, with no Draw. See [Refunds and Reclaim](/docs/refunds).
</Q>

<Q q="Which cards can I buy?">
  Gift cards for thousands of brands in many countries. Each works only in its own country, in its currency, unless it's marked **Works worldwide**, so pick your country in the [shop](/shop) first. See [Buy a card](/docs/buy).
</Q>

<Q q="How fast does the code arrive?">
  Usually within a minute. It shows on the page you bought from, in [Purchases](/purchases), and in your email. See [Your cards and codes](/docs/buy/your-cards).
</Q>

<Q q="What's the smallest card I can buy?">
  Purchases start at $0.50. Smaller amounts show dashed on a card's page.
</Q>

<Q q="Is Luckout part of Bitrefill?">
  No. Luckout sources its gift cards from Bitrefill, which isn't affiliated with Luckout.
</Q>

<Q q="Can I return a card I've received?">
  Once a delivery is confirmed, the purchase is final: the contract runs the Draw, and the card can't be refunded through Luckout. If a code doesn't work, see [Get help](/docs/support#a-code-doesnt-work).
</Q>

## Modes and cashback

<Q q="How does Luckout make money?">
  Every lucky mode is built so that, across many purchases, buyers pay 105.6% of the price on average. That 5.6% is Luckout's only revenue. Normal purchases cost exactly the price. See [How Luckout stays fair](/docs/fairness).
</Q>

<Q q="Which mode should I pick?">
  Normal if you just want the card at its price. The lucky modes all average the same over many purchases. They differ in how much extra you pay up front and how often cashback comes back: Lite costs least up front and pays least often, Jackpot costs most and pays most often, and Classic sits between. See [The four modes](/docs/modes).
</Q>

<Q q="Can a lucky mode leave me paying more than the price?">
  Yes, when there's no cashback: you've paid the price plus that mode's extra. Any cashback at all puts you below the price. No cashback is the single most likely result in every lucky mode.
</Q>

<Q q="What's the most cashback I can get?">
  All of what you paid: the card is free. One purchase can get at most the Cashback Cap, so on a big card only part of the price plays. See [Big cards](/docs/modes#big-cards).
</Q>

<Q q="How random is the Draw?">
  It uses NEAR's block randomness, mixed with your purchase number, in a step that runs a block after delivery is confirmed, so nobody can retry it. Anyone can recompute it from its Draw Proof. See [The Draw](/docs/draw).
</Q>

## Your money

<Q q="Where is my money while it's in Luckout?">
  In the Luckout contract on NEAR, credited to your account, not in a company wallet. You can withdraw anything that isn't in a purchase at any time. See [Your balance](/docs/balance).
</Q>

<Q q="Are there fees?">
  No top-up, service or withdrawal fees. Luckout pays the NEAR network fee for your actions, within a daily number of free ones. When money is converted between chains, the conversion's spread comes out of the amount, and your wallet pays the sending chain's fee.
</Q>

<Q q="Which wallets work?">
  NEAR wallets like HOT, Meteor and Intear; EVM wallets like MetaMask, Rabby and Coinbase Wallet; and Solana wallets like Phantom, Solflare and Backpack. See [Create your account](/docs/sign-in).
</Q>

<Q q="Do I need NEAR?">
  Not with an EVM or Solana wallet: Luckout pays the NEAR network fees for you. Some NEAR wallets pay their own small fee.
</Q>

<Q q="What if a purchase is stuck?">
  If Luckout neither delivers nor refunds it within 24 hours, you can take the full amount back yourself with **Reclaim it**. See [Reclaim](/docs/refunds#reclaim).
</Q>

## Telegram

<Q q="Can the Telegram bot spend my money?">
  Only when you tap its Buy button, and never above the per-purchase and daily limits you set. The contract enforces those limits on every purchase, whoever sends it. See [How the bot keeps you safe](/docs/telegram/safety).
</Q>

<Q q="Can I use the bot in a group?">
  No. It answers only in your private chat, so your codes stay there.
</Q>
