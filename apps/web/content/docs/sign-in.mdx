Your Luckout account is the crypto wallet you sign in with. There's no password to make and no form to fill in: you sign one message in your wallet, which is free and sends no transaction.

## Wallets that work

<Tiles>
  <Tile title="NEAR wallets" tint="sky">HOT, Meteor, Intear and the others in NEAR Connect. HOT also works inside Telegram.</Tile>
  <Tile title="EVM wallets" tint="lilac">MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet and more. No NEAR needed.</Tile>
  <Tile title="Solana wallets" tint="lime">Phantom, Solflare, Backpack and more. No NEAR needed.</Tile>
</Tiles>

Each wallet is its own Luckout account, with its own balance, limits and purchases. With an EVM or Solana wallet you never need NEAR: Luckout pays the NEAR network fee for your deposits, withdrawals and settings, within a daily number of free actions. [Your balance](/docs/balance#network-fees) has the details.

## Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Sign in" tint="sky">It's at the top of every page in the app.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose the kind of wallet">NEAR wallet, EVM wallet or Solana wallet, then pick yours from the list.</Step>
  <Step title="Sign the message" tint="gold">Your wallet shows a sign-in message from Luckout. Signing it proves the wallet is yours. It costs nothing and moves no money.</Step>
</Steps>

You stay signed in on that device for 7 days. If you close your wallet before signing, nothing is shared with Luckout.

## The Welcome steps

The first time you sign in, Welcome sets up the rest. It takes about a minute, and you can skip it and do each part later on your [wallet page](/wallet).

1. **Choose a mode.** How you like to pay: Normal, Lite, Classic or Jackpot. It becomes your [default mode](#your-default-mode).
2. **Your email.** Where your cards are sent. See [below](#your-email-for-cards).
3. **Add funds.** Skipped if your account already has funds. See [Add funds](/docs/add-funds).
4. **Ready.** Your balance, default mode and spending limits, and a button to [connect Telegram](/docs/telegram).

## Your default mode

Every purchase uses your default mode unless you pick another one for that card, on the site or in Telegram. Welcome suggests Classic. If you never choose, it's Normal: the card at its price.

The mode you pick in Welcome is saved to your account with your first deposit. Until then it's kept only in that browser.

To change it later, open your [wallet page](/wallet) and choose another under **Default mode**. That takes one approval in your wallet. [The four modes](/docs/modes) compares them.

## Your email for cards

Each card you buy carries an email, your Checkout Email. Bitrefill emails each card's code and receipt there, and can reach you there about a code that doesn't work. A throwaway address is fine.

- **Where to give it:** in Welcome, or on a card's page, which asks for it before your first card.
- **Where to change it:** your [wallet page](/wallet), under **Email for your cards**.
- **What it's used for:** your cards' orders, and counting Bitrefill's [limits for each email](/docs/limits#bitrefills-limits). We store it encrypted.
- **When it isn't needed:** if you [connect your own Bitrefill account](/docs/limits#connect-bitrefill), your cards are bought on it instead.

Luckout sources its gift cards from Bitrefill, which isn't affiliated with Luckout.

## Signing out

Open the account menu at the top of the app and choose **Sign out**. **Sign out everywhere** signs out every device signed in to this account, this one included. Either way, your balance and purchases stay as they are.

<Note title="Using another wallet">
  Sign out, then sign in with the other wallet. It's a separate account, so it starts with its own empty balance.
</Note>
