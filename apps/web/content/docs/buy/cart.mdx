The Cart lets you buy up to 15 cards with one payment. Mix brands, amounts and modes: each card is still its own purchase, with its own code, its own Draw in a lucky mode, and its own refund if it can't be delivered.

<CartSplit />

## Fill it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add cards" tint="sky">On a card's page, choose the amount, the mode and how many, then tap **Add to cart**. The cart icon at the top shows how many cards are in it.</Step>
  <Step title="Check it">Open the cart to change how many of each, remove one, or change a card's mode on the [cart page](/cart). It shows the total and, for lucky cards, how much of it is the extra for a chance at cashback.</Step>
  <Step title="Pay once" tint="gold">Tap **Checkout**, then **Pay**. The whole amount comes out of your balance in one go, and your cards follow on one page.</Step>
</Steps>

Fifteen cards is the most one Cart holds, counting each copy: three of one card and twelve of another make a full Cart. It lives in your browser, so signing out clears it.

## How a Cart is bought

- **All or nothing at the start.** The Cart is accepted as a whole, or not at all, and nothing is charged if it isn't.
- **Then card by card.** A card that's slow doesn't hold up the others, and one that can't be delivered goes back to your balance in full, while the rest arrive as usual.
- **Each lucky card has its own Draw,** with its own random result, and its own [Draw Proof](/docs/draw#draw-proofs).

## Prices in a Cart

Each card is priced on its own, and the Cart page keeps the prices fresh. If a price rises before you pay, you're asked to confirm again, and you're never charged more than the button showed.

<Note title="Lucky cards in a big Cart">
  The cards in a Cart share the Cashback Reserve, each priced on what the cards before it leave. On a big Cart, a later lucky card may play only part of its price, or be bought at the price in Normal. The Cart says so before you pay.
</Note>

## If you can't check out

- **Your balance is short:** add funds for the difference, shown on the button.
- **A card is over your limits:** your per-purchase limit applies to each card, and your daily limit to the whole Cart. See [Limits](/docs/limits).
- **More than 15 of your cards are still in progress:** wait for them to finish, then check out.
- **Bitrefill's daily limit for your email:** the Cart can have only as many cards as your email has left today. See [Bitrefill's limits](/docs/limits#bitrefills-limits).

Luckout sources its gift cards from Bitrefill, which isn't affiliated with Luckout.
