## A card that can't be delivered

If a card can't be delivered, Luckout marks it failed, and the full amount you paid, including any extra for a lucky mode, goes straight back to your balance. There's no Draw. The purchase shows **Refunded**, with the reason, such as "Bitrefill couldn't deliver this voucher." If Telegram is linked, the bot tells you too.

In a [Cart](/docs/buy/cart), only that card comes back. The others are delivered as usual.

Luckout sources its gift cards from Bitrefill, which isn't affiliated with Luckout.

## A card that's getting ready

Now and then a card shows **Getting it ready** while Luckout prepares to buy it. If you haven't been charged yet, the page says so, and you can tap **Cancel** until it's ready: "Cancelled. You weren't charged."

If it isn't ready in time, Luckout cancels it without a charge, or, if you were charged, refunds the full amount before Reclaim would open.

## Reclaim

If Luckout has neither delivered nor refunded a purchase 24 hours after you bought it, the contract lets you take the full amount back yourself. Only you can, and only for your own purchase.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the purchase" tint="sky">In [Purchases](/purchases). After 10 minutes in progress, it says **Taking longer than usual** and when Reclaim opens.</Step>
  <Step title="Tap Reclaim it">Once 24 hours have passed, the purchase says the full amount is yours to take back.</Step>
  <Step title="Approve in your wallet" tint="gold">The full amount you paid is back in your balance. Luckout pays the network fee.</Step>
</Steps>

## Top-Up refunds

If a [Top-Up from another chain](/docs/add-funds/other-chains) doesn't go through, 1Click sends the money back. Change from a Top-Up you overpaid comes back the same way.

- **To the wallet that paid,** for an EVM or Solana wallet paying on Arbitrum, Base, Polygon, BNB Chain or Solana.
- **To your NEAR Intents account** in every other case. It's your own account in NEAR Intents, controlled only by your wallet: Luckout can move money out of it only with your signature.

### Send it back to your wallet

Money in your NEAR Intents account shows on your [wallet page](/wallet) under **Also yours, in NEAR Intents**. Tap **Send to my wallet** (or **Send to my NEAR wallet as USDC**) and approve once. It's converted on 1Click and sent to your own wallet, and Luckout pays the network fee. With an EVM or Solana wallet, you can spend it instead: move it into your balance with the **From NEAR Intents** tab under **Add funds**.

<Note title="A delivered card is final">
  Once a delivery is confirmed, the contract runs the Draw and the purchase can't be refunded through Luckout. If a code doesn't work, see [Get help](/docs/support#a-code-doesnt-work).
</Note>
