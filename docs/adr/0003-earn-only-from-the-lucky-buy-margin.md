# Earn only from the Lucky Buy margin

The only revenue is the 5.6% Target Gross Margin built into every Game Version (ADR-0002). There is no Top-Up fee, no per-purchase service fee, and nothing hidden in the Reference Price. Running costs are model calls for the Agent, swap fees for refilling the operator's Bitrefill balance, NEAR gas for server-submitted purchases, and hosting; at a 5.6% margin a ₹500 Lucky Buy is expected to earn ₹28, which covers them.

Standard Purchases (the Normal mode) are kept at zero margin. Each one costs the operator a model conversation, a fulfillment, and a share of the Bitrefill float while earning nothing; they are accepted as the cost of letting customers use one Balance and one Agent for everything, and of giving risk-averse customers a reason to stay.

The margin is an expectation, not a per-purchase guarantee. For 100 Draws, the operator still loses money about 0% of the time on Lite, 1% on Classic, and 12% on Jackpot; over 1,000 Draws it practically never does. The Cashback Reserve absorbs that early variance, so it is funded before launch.

The margin is not split per purchase. A fixed cut, such as 1% to the operator and 4.6% to the reserve, would pay the operator during losing streaks and drain the reserve when it most needs the money. Instead, all of the margin stays in the Cashback Reserve until Free Reserve reaches a target, initially 1,000 USDC. At that size the Cashback Cap's starting fixed 50 USDC, not its 20% share, limits each purchase, and every Game Version applies in full to vouchers up to about 33 USDC (Jackpot) to 45 USDC (Lite). The operator withdraws only Free Reserve above the target, monthly, to pay running costs, and keeps the rest as profit. The target is raised as customers buy larger vouchers. It is an operating policy, not a contract rule: customer safety comes from each Lucky Buy's locked liability, not from the reserve's size.
